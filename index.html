Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about GiftBuyer.

How can I sell my gift card?
Select the gift card you have and contact GiftBuyer support through the available contact channel. Our team will guide you through the verification and selling process.
How can I check today's gift card rates?
You can check the latest available buying rates in the Gift Buyer Buying Rates section on this page.
Which gift cards are supported?
Supported brands are displayed in the Gift Cards section. The supported list can change over time.
How can I check my payment status?
Use the Payment Status button from the website navigation to open the payment status page.
Do you buy gaming gift cards?
GiftBuyer may support selected gaming gift cards such as PlayStation, Roblox and Valorant. Please check the current supported gift card list before contacting us.
Do you buy retail gift cards?
GiftBuyer may support selected retail gift cards such as Flipkart, Bata and Croma. Availability and buying rates may change, so always check the latest information.
How is the gift card price decided?
The buying price can depend on the gift card brand, denomination, region, validity, demand and verification. Always check the latest rate before selling.
What information is required to sell a gift card?
Depending on the card and verification process, GiftBuyer may ask for relevant gift card details and payment details. Never share sensitive information outside the official GiftBuyer support channel.

About GiftBuyer

A simple platform for gift card buyers and sellers.

GiftBuyer is an online platform focused on gift card buying and selling. The website allows users to explore supported gift cards and check available buying rates.

Why use GiftBuyer?

GiftBuyer is designed to make the gift card selling process simple and easy to understand. Users can check supported cards, view rates and contact support from one place.

Check rates before selling

Gift card buying rates can change. Always check the latest rate shown on the website before starting a transaction.

Supported Gift Card Categories

GiftBuyer may support both gaming and retail gift cards, including selected PlayStation, Roblox, Valorant, Flipkart, Bata and Croma gift cards.
